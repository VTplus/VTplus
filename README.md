# Mathias Müller

🛠️ My work focuses on virtual reality for healthcare, multimodal interaction,
and AI-assisted therapeutic and research workflows. This includes
therapist-controlled interactive virtual environments, experimental control,
synchronised multimodal data acquisition, eye tracking, behavioural and
physiological measurement, and biosignal integration.

🤖 I'm also developing and evaluating tools based on local LLMs,
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and AI agents for internal knowledge
and quality management. This work focuses on source-grounded information
retrieval, document review and knowledge curation, with controlled access
to technical documentation, workflows and regulatory knowledge.

🔬 My research interests include human experience in virtual environments,
mental health, neurorehabilitation, EEG/EMG integration, brain-computer
interfaces (BCI), and neurofeedback.

## ⚕️ VTplus — Medical VR Platform & AI-Assisted Workflows in Healthcare

At [VTplus](https://www.vtplus.eu/en/), we develop a modular virtual reality
platform for clinical applications and empirical research. It combines
simulation software, interactive environments, hardware integration
and interfaces to measurement, interaction and stimulation systems.

- **Simulation and control:** Configurable scenarios, stimulus and session
  control, and therapist or researcher interfaces, with support for VR
  headsets and projection systems such as PowerWall and CAVE.

- **Multimodal integration:** Synchronised acquisition of interaction,
  movement, gaze and physiological data, with interfaces for EEG/EMG,
  brain-computer interfaces, neurofeedback and external stimulation.

- **Adaptive methods:** Research and development in multimodal data fusion,
  virtual agents, AI-assisted intervention support and biosignal-driven
  feedback loops.

For clinical applications, our development process takes applicable medical
device requirements into account, alongside usability, system integration
and technical documentation.
